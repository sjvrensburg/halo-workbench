Rewriting style, and spans to protect

Style applies when both sides are the same language.

A guide is any markdown file of editorial rules — a skill's SKILL.md works as one. It runs in two passes: the guide is applied to list what is wrong, then the rewrite fixes exactly that, and you see the list.

Citations, LaTeX, maths, numbers and escapes are protected automatically. Protecting a span means do not translate it — so protecting 「新华社」 in a Chinese→English job leaves 「新华社」 in the English.
