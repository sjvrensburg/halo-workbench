3 · The reading

Ask another model

Different inductive biases agreeing is evidence about the ink. One model sampled five times agreeing only means it is confident, which it can be while wrong. Each of these is started when you press it and released after a few minutes idle.

What this is not

Not a transcription service, not a marking aid, and not a batch pipeline. The literature measures over-correction — models silently repairing student spelling — in 42–66% of transcriptions across 15 VLMs (arXiv 2604.22774), and explicit faithfulness prompting buys about four points and no net gain. That risk belongs to batch work, where a transcript is produced now and marked later and nobody re-reads the ink. Here you are beside the transcript, not downstream of it.

Nothing captured on this page is written to disk. There is no job directory, no retention window and no log of any reading — the image lives in this tab and, for the length of one request, in the app's memory.
