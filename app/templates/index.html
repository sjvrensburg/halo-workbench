Specialist local-model tasks on the EVO-X3. Every one ships a verifier that runs on every request — this box is good at narrow work with a cheap automatic check, and bad at anything judged by eye. On the fifth the verifier is you, because you are looking at the same ink at the same moment.

  • PDF → markdown marker, with its checksummed provenance sidecar as the proof
  • Assisted redaction candidates you confirm; the output is re-read to prove they are gone
  • Regex from examples never returned until it has run against every example you gave
  • Translate & rewrite zh↔en, or the same language twice to rewrite in place
  • Handwriting hint several readings of one squiggle, with where the models part company

Jobs are directories under ~/.local/state/halo-workbench/jobs/ and are pruned after 7 days. One job runs at a time: everything here shares a GPU with three resident model servers. The hint tool is the exception in both respects — it is interactive rather than queued, and nothing it captures reaches the disk at all.
